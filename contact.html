§ 04 — BEFORE YOU WRITE

Common questions.

Do you take projects smaller than a full system rebuild?

Yes. Standalone work like a single database migration, a landing page or a brand refresh runs under the Project package described on the services page.

Can one team handle both the design and the engineering?

That's the point of working across all five disciplines — the same team carries a project from analysis through to a shipped, designed interface.

What do you need from us before a quote?

A short description of the problem, rough timeline, and access to anyone who knows the current process — that's usually enough for a first estimate.

Do you work with teams outside your time zone?

Yes, the studio is remote-first and most client communication happens asynchronously in writing, with calls scheduled around your hours.
