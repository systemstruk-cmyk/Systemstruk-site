§ 01 — ABOUT

We think in systems.

Systemstruk started as a two-person database consultancy and grew into a five-discipline studio — without ever losing the habit of mapping a problem before touching a keyboard.

Most technical problems aren't technical. They're a business process nobody wrote down, running on a spreadsheet nobody trusts.

We started in 2018 fixing exactly that — untangling databases for small operations teams who'd outgrown their own tools. Clients kept asking who could also design the interface, or build the site sitting on top of it. So we learned. Then we hired people who already knew.

Eight years on, the questions haven't changed. We still start every engagement by asking how the work actually gets done today, before we propose how it should. The five disciplines we now offer exist because that question kept leading somewhere new — never because we decided in advance what a "full-service studio" should look like.

What hasn't changed is the standard: a system is only finished when someone outside our team can read it, run it and extend it without calling us first.

§ 02 — PRINCIPLES

How we make decisions.

Five habits that shape every engagement, regardless of which discipline it starts in.

01

Structure before speed

We map a process before we design or build against it. A fast wrong answer costs more than a week of analysis.

02

One team, no handoffs

The person who scoped your system is reachable throughout the build — not replaced by a new face at each stage.

03

Documentation is a deliverable

A system your team can't read is a system you depend on us for forever. We write it down so you don't have to call.

04

Legible over clever

The simplest structure that solves the problem beats the impressive one, every time it comes down to a choice.

05

Measured by what stays live

We track how many of our systems are still in production years later, not just how many shipped on launch day.
